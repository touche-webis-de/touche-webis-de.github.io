Synopsis/Call

Decision-making and opinion-forming are everyday tasks that involve weighing pro and con arguments for or against different options. Our goal is to foster the development of technologies that support people in decision-making and opinion-forming and to improve our understanding of these processes. We invite you to participate in the 8th Touché lab on argumentation at CLEF 2027 featuring three tasks.

Shared Tasks

  • Human Value Detection
  • Generalizability of Argument Identification in Context
